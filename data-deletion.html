User Data Deletion Instructions

Application: Vird Auto-Pilot (Meta App ID: 3292835964437949)
Last Updated: September 20, 2026
According to Meta Platform Rules and GDPR/CCPA regulations, you have the right to request the deletion of all data associated with your Facebook / Instagram account that was processed by Vird Auto-Pilot.

How to Request Data Deletion

You can remove your data and access permissions at any time through either of the two methods below:

Method 1: Direct In-Facebook Removal (Recommended)

You can instantly revoke permissions and delete associated app data directly from your Facebook account settings:

  1. Log in to your Facebook profile.
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy → Settings.
  3. In the left menu, click on Apps and Websites (or visit facebook.com/settings?tab=applications).
  4. Find Vird Auto-Pilot in the list of authorized applications.
  5. Click Remove.
  6. Check the box that asks if you want Facebook to notify the developer to delete all your past activity data, then confirm by clicking Remove.

Method 2: Contact Administrator for Complete Manual Deletion

If you would like our team to immediately purge all stored records, tokens, Page identifiers, and cached metrics from our database:

  1. Send an email to: labrujasiete@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: [Data Deletion Request] Vird Auto-Pilot
  3. Include your Facebook Page Name or Page ID.

Once received, your request will be executed within 48 hours. You will receive an email confirmation once all database records and cached tokens have been completely wiped.

What Happens When Data is Deleted?

  • All stored OAuth access tokens for your Page are immediately destroyed.
  • All historical performance snapshots, reach data, and post logs associated with your account are permanently erased.
  • No scheduled posts or publishing jobs will execute for your account.
